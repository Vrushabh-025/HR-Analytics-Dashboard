# HR-Analytics-Dashboard
<img width="1307" height="731" alt="Screenshot " src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e742ce30-8ed3-49eb-8996-bf0b360521bc" />

This project analyzes employee data using Microsoft Power BI to understand employee attrition and identify patterns across different workforce characteristics. The dashboard examines attrition in relation to employee demographics, job roles, education, salary levels, gender, age, and years of experience with the company.

The goal of the analysis is to transform employee-level HR data into an interactive dashboard that helps identify groups with higher attrition and provides a clearer view of workforce retention patterns.

The dashboard includes key HR metrics such as Total Employees, Total Attrition, Attrition Rate, Average Age, Average Salary, and Average Years at Company.

It also provides interactive analysis of attrition by education, age group, salary level, job role, years at company, and gender, with a department filter that allows users to explore workforce patterns across different areas of the organization.

The company had an overall attrition rate of 16.1%, with 237 employees leaving out of 1,470. Younger employees and employees with fewer years at the company had higher attrition.Employees in lower salary groups had higher attrition, while Sales Representatives had one of the highest attrition rates.
The dashboard also shows attrition patterns by age, gender, education, salary, job role, and years at the company, helping identify groups with higher employee turnover.
